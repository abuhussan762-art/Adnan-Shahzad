# Product Requirements

## 1. User Input & Data Handling
- Upload existing CV (PDF/DOCX).
- Extract: personal info, experience, education, skills, certifications, projects, and locations.
- Preserve factual correctness; no hallucinations.
- Ask clarification questions only for missing or ambiguous data.
- Allow manual add/edit for all sections.

## 2. Structured Resume Profile
- Professional Summary
- Work Experience (role, company, location, dates, achievements)
- Skills (technical, soft, tools, software)
- Education
- Certifications & Licenses
- Projects (optional)
- Keywords Bank (industry-specific)

## 3. JD-Based Customization (Core)
- User pastes a job description.
- AI analyzes required skills, keywords, responsibilities, ATS signals.
- CV is tailored to align with the JD:
  - Rewrite summary aligned with role.
  - Reprioritize bullets based on relevance.
  - Inject keywords naturally.
  - Remove irrelevant or low-value content.
- Must remain truthful; no false experience.

## 4. ATS-Friendly Resume Rules
- No tables, text boxes, icons, or decorative elements.
- Simple section headings.
- Reverse chronological order.
- Fonts: Calibri, Arial, Helvetica, Times New Roman.
- Font size: 10.5–12 body, 13–15 headings.
- Margins: balanced and clean.
- Black/dark gray text only.
- Bullet points optimized for parsing.

## 5. Template System
- Corporate / Executive
- Engineering & Technical
- Safety Officer / HSE
- IT & Software
- Fresh Graduate
- All templates are minimal, professional, and consistent across PDF/DOCX.

## 6. AI Writing Standards
- Clear, concise, professional English.
- Action verbs with impact and metrics where possible.
- STAR-style, achievement-based bullets.
- Neutral, corporate tone aligned with international hiring standards.

## 7. Output Formats
- Editable Word (.docx)
- ATS-optimized PDF
- Formatting consistency across formats.

## 8. Version Control
- Save original CV and each JD-customized version.
- Allow comparison, re-edit, and re-export.

## 9. Advanced Features
- ATS score estimate against JD.
- Keyword match percentage.
- Red-flag detection (gaps, weak bullets, overused words).
- Suggestions panel: skills to add, certification recommendations.
- Optional: cover letter generator and LinkedIn optimization.

## 10. Ethical & Professional Constraints
- Do not fabricate experience, education, or certifications.
- Maintain user privacy and professional integrity.
