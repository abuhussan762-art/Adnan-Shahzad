# Templates & Formatting Standards

## Global Formatting Rules
- No tables, text boxes, icons, or decorative elements.
- Simple section headings with consistent hierarchy.
- Reverse chronological order.
- Fonts: Calibri, Arial, Helvetica, Times New Roman.
- Font size: 10.5–12 body, 13–15 headings.
- Margins: clean and balanced.
- Black/dark gray text only.
- Bullets optimized for ATS parsing.

## Template Variants

### Corporate / Executive
- Emphasis on leadership, strategy, and cross-functional impact.
- Summary with executive-level keywords and scope.

### Engineering & Technical
- Emphasis on technical skills, systems, and measurable outcomes.
- Skills section placed near the top.

### Safety Officer / HSE
- Emphasis on compliance, audits, incident reduction, and safety metrics.
- Certifications and licenses prominently displayed.

### IT & Software
- Emphasis on technical stack, tools, and delivery metrics.
- Projects section included by default.

### Fresh Graduate
- Emphasis on education, projects, internships, and transferable skills.
- Summary highlights learning agility and coursework.

## Export Requirements
- DOCX and PDF outputs must render identically.
- Ensure line wrapping and spacing remain consistent across formats.
